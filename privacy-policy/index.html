Privacy Policy

Fumeto Reader Plus (package name com.fumeto.reader) for Android
Effective date: July 28, 2026

The short version: Fumeto Reader Plus is a local-first app. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking, and the developer operates no servers that receive your data. Your comics, reading progress, settings, and translations stay on your device. The app only communicates over the network with services you explicitly configure — such as your own media server or your own translation API account.

Who we are

Fumeto Reader Plus is developed and published by an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, contact: fumetoreaderdev@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Comic files and libraries you import or create, and their metadata;
  • Reading progress and library organization;
  • App settings and preferences;
  • Translations generated for your pages;
  • Optional server addresses and credentials you enter for your own media server (see below);
  • Optional API keys you enter for a translation provider (see below);
  • Local diagnostic logs (see “Diagnostics and error reports”).

None of this is transmitted to the developer. You can delete this data at any time by removing items in the app, clearing the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Network features you control

All network features are optional and disabled until you configure them. When you use them, data goes only to the destination you chose:

  • Your own media server. If you connect a self-hosted comic server (such as Komga, Kavita, or YACReaderLibraryServer), the address and credentials or API key you enter are stored on your device and sent only to that server, in order to browse and fetch your comics from it. The developer never receives them.
  • Cloud translation with your own API key. The app can translate pages entirely on your device. If you instead choose a cloud-based translation model and provide your own API key (for example, an OpenRouter account), then the text extracted from the pages you choose to translate is sent to that provider under your own account and is subject to that provider’s privacy policy. Your key is stored on your device and sent only to that provider. If you only use on-device translation, no page content ever leaves your device.
  • Component downloads. On-device translation components (model files) are downloaded from static file hosting when you request them. As with any download, the hosting service can see standard web request metadata such as your IP address. No personal content is sent.

Diagnostics and error reports

The app keeps a small, recent log of technical errors on your device to help diagnose problems. It is never transmitted automatically. If you choose to copy a diagnostic report to share (for example, when reporting a bug), the report is designed to contain only technical information — app version, device model, error messages — and to exclude comic titles, file names, page content, and any credentials or API keys.

Purchases

If the app offers paid upgrades, purchases are processed by Google Play’s billing system. The developer receives confirmation of what was purchased in order to unlock the corresponding features, and never receives your payment details. Google’s handling of payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don’t do

  • No user accounts or sign-in;
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs or identifiers;
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking;
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — the developer holds no user data to sell or share;
  • No collection of location, contacts, or other device data unrelated to reading comics.

Permissions

The app requests network access for the optional features described above, and reads comic files that you explicitly select or import through the Android file picker.

Children

Fumeto Reader Plus is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal data, requests to access or delete data are fulfilled directly on your device: all app data can be viewed inside the app and removed by deleting items, clearing app data, or uninstalling. For anything else, contact the email address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change — for example, if an optional cloud service operated by the developer is introduced — this policy will be updated at this address with a new effective date before those changes take effect.
